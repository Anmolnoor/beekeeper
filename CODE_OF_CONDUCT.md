# Beekeeper Code of Conduct

Beekeeper is a small project that takes governance and audit seriously — the
same standards apply to how we behave with each other. This document tells you
what's expected, what's not, and how to report a problem.

It is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant][homepage] v2.1 with project-
specific guidance for an agent-runtime community where AI-assisted contributions
are the norm and the maintainer pool is small.

## Our Pledge

We — maintainers and contributors — pledge to make participation in the
Beekeeper community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, technically
honest, and healthy community.

## Where This Applies

This Code of Conduct applies in all official Beekeeper spaces:

- This repository: issues, pull requests, discussions, code review comments,
  and commit messages.
- Any official chat, mailing list, or event held under the Beekeeper name.
- Any time you are publicly representing the project (talks, blog posts,
  social posts using the Beekeeper name or logo).

It also applies to private interactions when those interactions affect the
safety or experience of someone in the community.

## Behavior We Want

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences — even
  when you disagree with their architecture taste.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Accepting responsibility, apologizing to those affected by mistakes, and
  learning from them.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the community, not just your
  own pet feature.
- Writing in your own voice. AI assistance is welcome; AI ghostwriting that
  pretends to be you is not.

## Behavior We Don't Want

The standard Contributor Covenant prohibitions apply, plus a few project-
specific ones that come up often in agent-framework communities:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political
  attacks — including against other agent frameworks, their authors, or their
  contributors. Compare honestly; do not bash.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information (physical address, email, employer,
  unpublished work) without their explicit permission.
- **Mass-automated tracker abuse.** Submitting large volumes of agent-generated
  issues or PRs, opening throwaway accounts to bypass the contribution gate
  in `CONTRIBUTING.md`, or coordinating with others to do so. This is treated
  as harassment of the maintainer.
- **Pretending an AI-generated PR is your own work.** Submitting code you have
  not read, cannot explain, and would not be able to debug. This is a quality
  issue handled by `CONTRIBUTING.md`; doing it deliberately and at scale is a
  conduct issue handled here.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional engineering setting.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

The project maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of
Conduct and will take appropriate corrective action in response to any behavior
they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

The maintainer has the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject
comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, discussions, and other
contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and will
communicate reasons for moderation decisions when reasonable.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code, report it
privately to:

- **Email:** `conduct@anmolnoor.com`
- **GitHub Security Advisories** (if the report involves a private repository
  or sensitive information):
  <https://github.com/Anmolnoor/beekeeper/security/advisories/new>

Please include:

- What happened, with as much specificity as you are comfortable sharing.
- Where it happened (link to issue/PR/discussion if applicable).
- Any other people affected.
- Whether you have already responded and what response you got.

You will receive an acknowledgement within **7 days**. The maintainer is
obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter.

> Security vulnerabilities have a separate channel — see `SECURITY.md`. Code
> quality complaints have a separate channel — see `CONTRIBUTING.md`. This
> document is for conduct.

## Enforcement Guidelines

These are the consequences the maintainer will consider for any action they
deem in violation of this Code of Conduct.

### 1. Correction

**Impact:** Use of inappropriate language or other behavior deemed
unprofessional or unwelcome.

**Consequence:** A private written warning, providing clarity around the
violation and why the behavior was inappropriate. A public apology may be
requested.

### 2. Warning

**Impact:** A violation through a single incident or series of actions.

**Consequence:** A warning with consequences for continued behavior. No
interaction with the people involved — including unsolicited interaction with
those enforcing the Code — for a specified period. Violating these terms may
lead to a temporary or permanent ban.

### 3. Temporary Ban

**Impact:** A serious violation of community standards, including sustained
inappropriate behavior or sustained tracker abuse.

**Consequence:** A temporary ban from all forms of interaction or public
communication with the community for a specified period. No public or private
contact with the people involved is allowed during this period. Violating
these terms may lead to a permanent ban.

### 4. Permanent Ban

**Impact:** Demonstrating a pattern of violation of community standards,
sustained inappropriate behavior, harassment of an individual, aggression
toward or disparagement of classes of individuals, or repeated mass-automated
abuse of the tracker.

**Consequence:** A permanent ban from any form of interaction within the
Beekeeper community. As stated in `CONTRIBUTING.md`: no takesies-backsies.
